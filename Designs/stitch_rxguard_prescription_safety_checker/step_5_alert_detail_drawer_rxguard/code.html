1. Why It Matters (Clinical Pharmacokinetics)

Ciprofloxacin is a potent CYP1A2 inhibitor and moderately inhibits CYP3A4, significantly suppressing R-warfarin hepatic metabolic clearance while simultaneously disrupting intestinal vitamin K-producing microflora synthesis.

crisis_alert
Kinetic Impact: This synergy dramatically elevates free warfarin plasma concentration, increasing INR unpredictably within 48–72 hours. This geriatric patient (64y, male) with reduced renal reserve faces severe risk of major hemorrhagic complications (gastrointestinal hemorrhage, large-muscle hematoma, or fatal intracranial hemorrhage).

Prothrombin time and INR should be monitored rigorously upon co-administration and dose of warfarin adjusted empirically by 30% to 50% or alternative antimicrobial selected.”

3. Recommended Clinical Action

contact_phone Contact prescriber / pharmacist; do not independently stop, start or change medication.
1
Switch to non-interacting alternative: Recommend pathogen-directed antimicrobial such as Ceftriaxone (if inpatient IV indicated and penicillin skin tolerance verified) or oral Nitrofurantoin / TMP-SMX (calibrated to renal clearance and local urology antibiogram).
2
If Ciprofloxacin is essential: Empirically reduce daily warfarin dose by 30% to 50%. Order immediate baseline INR and scheduled 48-hour repeat INR assay. Explicitly counsel patient on reporting epistaxis, spontaneous hematoma, dark tarry stools, or frank hematuria.
