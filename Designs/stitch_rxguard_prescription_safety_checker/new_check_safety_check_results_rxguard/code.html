High Severity CDS-DDI-CYP1A2-0842 Level 1 Contraindicated Combination

CYP-Mediated Drug Interaction: Ciprofloxacin + Warfarin

expand_more
chips Pharmacokinetic Mechanism & Clinical Hazard

Ciprofloxacin strongly inhibits hepatic CYP1A2 and moderately inhibits CYP3A4, drastically reducing clearance of active (R)- and (S)-Warfarin enantiomers. Concomitant use can precipitate rapid INR escalation (>6.0) within 48–72 hours, resulting in substantial risk of major intracranial or gastrointestinal hemorrhage.

recommend Recommended Clinician Actions
  • Consider non-quinolone alternative: Aztreonam 1g IV Q8H (monobactam safe in penicillin anaphylaxis) or discuss with Infectious Disease.
  • If Ciprofloxacin is deemed essential: Empirically reduce ongoing Warfarin dose by 30% to 50% starting day 1, mandate INR draw at 48 hours, and instruct patient on immediate epistaxis/hematoma vigilance.
