## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a professional, trustworthy, and precise visual workspace tailored for clinical dental practitioners. The interface evokes calmness, clinical hygiene, and absolute reliability through clean surfaces, high legibility, and a restrained color palette. 

The visual style is grounded in a **Corporate / Modern** framework, emphasizing systematic utility, crisp data presentation, and clear hierarchical separation to support high-focus medical environments.

## Colors

The color system builds trust and clarity. Primary teal provides a distinct clinical anchor, while secondary and tertiary blues support secondary actions and contextual highlights. Neutral backgrounds ensure high contrast for dense patient records and imaging data.

## Typography

Using Inter throughout ensures uncompromising legibility across medical records, charts, and diagnostic dashboards. Type scales dynamically to support rapid scanning of clinical histories and treatment notes.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column fluid grid organizes complex patient data, scheduling modules, and odontogram views. Generous margins and consistent gutters prevent visual fatigue during long clinical shifts.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on **Tonal layers** combined with subtle, low-opacity outlines. Surfaces step forward using crisp containment and minimal shadow diffusion to maintain a clean, flat, yet dimensional workspace suitable for medical data entry.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft shape language (`roundedness` level 1), utilizing modest radii (0.25rem to 0.75rem) on cards, inputs, and buttons. This balances modern approachability with structured, professional rigor.

## Components

- **Buttons:** Primary teal actions, secondary outlined options, and high-contrast inverted states for critical workflows.
- **Inputs:** Clean search and data entry fields with clear label placement and subtle border highlights.
- **Cards:** Container surfaces holding modular patient information, odontogram tools, and appointment schedules.
- **Lists & Tables:** Dense, high-legibility rows optimized for patient histories and billing records.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** High-contrast selections for treatment plan approvals and diagnostic markers.
- **Chips & Badges:** Status indicators for appointment types, urgency levels, and insurance verification.
